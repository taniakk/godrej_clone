Overview

Spread across ~ 12,383 sq. mt. (~ 3.06 acres) in the heart of Vastrapur, Godrej Altus stands as a landmark of thoughtful design and quiet distinction. Expansive greens, curated amenities and sweeping skyline views come together to create a living experience defined by refinement and timeless appeal. From the moment you arrive, every detail reflects understated elegance—crafted to feel seamless, considered and complete.

Set across an expansive ~1,858 sq. mt. (~20,000 sq. ft.) of curated terrace, this rarefied realm reveals a collection of 10+ thoughtfully envisioned amenities, where sky and horizon merge seamlessly. Here, each space is composed in dialogue with the skyline—framing moments that feel both personal and infinite.

Godrej Altus is located at 11/1908, Sunteck MAXXWorld, Naigaon East Vasai Link Rd, Naigaon East, Mumbai, Tivri, Maharashtra 401208. Set within Mumbai's flourishing western corridor, it offers seamless connectivity to major highways, business districts, and metro corridors.
Godrej Altus offers three distinct configurations: 3 BHK Premium (3,076 sq.ft. salable area), 4 BHK Majestic (4,084–4,127 sq.ft. salable area), and 4 BHK Palatial (4,608–4,769 sq.ft. salable area). Each is a distinct interpretation of the Altus philosophy.
